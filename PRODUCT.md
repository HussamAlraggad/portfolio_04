# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Four confirmed audiences, all served by one site:

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating fit for internships and early-career roles. This is the primary audience: Hussam is a master's student with no industry work history yet.
- Freelance and contract clients evaluating whether to start a paid conversation.
- Peers and the dev community evaluating craft, thinking, and collaboration potential.
- Academic and admissions committees evaluating research potential and technical depth.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Hussam Alraggad: a fresh, self-contained presentation of his work, range, and design-engineering sensibility. It exists so a visitor can understand who he is and what he can do by exploring the site itself, without downloading a resume first. Success means the visitor explores the work and then contacts him, starts a conversation, or connects.

## Positioning

Design-engineering is the lead signal: frontend engineering and design first, AI software engineering second, full-stack engineering third. The site itself is part of the argument, a portfolio whose craft demonstrates the claimed craft. A generic developer portfolio could list the same skills; it could not truthfully make the interface itself the proof.

"AI software engineer" means engineering software with AI as leverage: agents and copilots inside a disciplined build process, with judgment, architecture, review, and verification still owned by the engineer. It explicitly does not mean vibe coding. The site must make that distinction legible without arguing about it.

## Operating Context

- GitHub is the confirmed evidence hub for code and shipped projects.
- Project evidence also lives in local files (CV, writeups, screenshots, design files) and in material Hussam will supply later.
- The site is evaluated in a browsing session; a visitor should understand the work without installing or downloading anything.
- The previous portfolio exists but is explicitly not a reference. This is a fresh start.
- The same site must read as credible to all four audiences without splitting into four separate sites.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Next.js 16.4.2 (App Router), React 19.3, Tailwind CSS v4, TypeScript, and pnpm; runs as a web app.
- Content must be real and sourced. Do not invent employers, clients, metrics, testimonials, or credentials.
- The portfolio must stand on its own without a resume download; a CV may still be offered as a secondary asset if one exists.
- Open decisions (do not invent): the public handle or alias value, the logo file, the GitHub handle or URL, the contact channel and email, the domain, and the exact project and case-study list.
- The logo and handle are confirmed brand assets but have not been supplied in this repo yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Public identity: the personal name Hussam Alraggad, used alongside a handle or alias (value to be supplied).
- A logo exists and is intended to become the enduring brand mark; the asset still needs to be supplied.
- The identity is new. The previous portfolio is not to be reused or visually referenced.
- No confirmed voice, tagline, or color commitment yet. Those are visual-world decisions for new-work, not init.

## Evidence on Hand

- CV and work history exist outside this repo.
- GitHub profile with live projects and repos (handle to be supplied).
- Case studies and visual material (writeups, screenshots, design files) exist locally; paths to be supplied.
- Some project material will be provided in later sessions.
- Absent and not to be fabricated: testimonials, client logos, published metrics, press, and academic publications unless Hussam supplies them.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the argument: every claim should resolve to a real project, artifact, or case study.
2. Design-engineering first: interface craft is the primary signal, not a generic skills list.
3. Self-contained by default: a visitor should understand Hussam without downloading anything.
4. Truth over volume: show fewer, better-documented projects rather than a padded list.
5. One coherent identity for four audiences: the same site must read as credible to a recruiter, a client, a peer, and a committee.
